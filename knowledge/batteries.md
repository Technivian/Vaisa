# VONROC Battery & Platform Compatibility

Sources: VONROC VPower 20V product pages (see `products/` for individual
tools) and https://vonroc.nl/accuboormachine-20v-incl-2-0ah-accu-en-snellader
Retrieved: 2026-08-20

VONROC cordless tools use one of **two separate battery platforms: 12V and
20V**. Batteries from one platform are **not** physically or electrically
compatible with tools or chargers from the other platform. Never tell a
customer that "all VONROC batteries work with all VONROC machines" — that
is incorrect.

## VONROC VPower 20V platform

- Batteries on this platform are interchangeable across all VPower 20V
  tools, in either **2.0Ah** or **4.0Ah** capacity — a higher Ah battery
  gives longer runtime, not more power.
- Confirmed VPower 20V tools in this demo's knowledge base: the CD510DC
  cordless drill, the CD511DC PRO cordless drill, and the RH501DC rotary
  hammer (see `products/`). All three explicitly state "VONROC VPower 20V
  platform" on their product pages and accept the same 2.0Ah/4.0Ah
  batteries and fast charger.

## VONROC 12V platform

- A separate, smaller platform for lighter-duty 12V tools. 12V batteries
  and chargers do not fit 20V tools, and vice versa.

## How to confirm which battery a customer needs

Ask for the exact tool model (printed on the rating label, and usually in
the product name, e.g. "CD510DC") if it isn't already clear — the platform
is not always obvious from a general description like "my drill" or "my
grinder". Only recommend a specific battery once the platform is confirmed
via the model number or an explicit "12V" / "20V" / "VPower 20V" marking.
If the tool model isn't in this knowledge base and the platform can't be
confirmed from what the customer has said, say so and offer to escalate
rather than guessing.
