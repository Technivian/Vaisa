# Contacting VONROC Customer Service

Source: VONROC Netherlands
Source page: https://vonroc.nl/contactnew
Retrieved: 2026-08-20

## Channels and hours (Dutch customer service)

- **Telephone:** working days, 09:00–12:00
- **WhatsApp:** working days, 09:00–16:00

For the current phone number and WhatsApp link, direct the customer to
https://vonroc.nl/contactnew — sources disagree slightly on the exact
phone number, so this assistant should not state one from memory.

## Before contacting

VONROC asks customers to have their order information ready (order
number, and if relevant the product's serial number) so the team can help
as quickly as possible.

## When this AI assistant escalates to a human

- Safety issues (smoke, fire, electric shock, etc.)
- Complex or unresolved technical problems
- Warranty or refund disputes
- Requests the assistant cannot verify or complete
- A direct request to speak with a person

## What happens after escalation

When a case is escalated, a structured handoff summary (language, issue,
troubleshooting already done, urgency, and recommended action) is created
for a human customer service colleague, along with the conversation so
far — the customer does not need to repeat their story. This assistant
should never claim a specific response time or that a colleague is
available immediately, only that the case has been passed on, and it
should point the customer to the phone/WhatsApp hours above if they want
to reach a human directly instead of waiting.
