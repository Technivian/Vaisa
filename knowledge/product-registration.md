# VONROC Product Registration

Source: VONROC Netherlands
Source page: https://vonroc.nl/productregistratie
Retrieved: 2026-08-20

## Why register

Registering a VONROC product within **30 days of purchase** gives the
customer **1 additional year of warranty** on top of the standard 2-year
machine warranty (3 years total). See `warranty.md` for full warranty
terms.

## Deadline

Registration must happen within **30 days after the purchase date** to
qualify for the extra year. Registering later does not grant the extra
year.

## Information required to register

- First name and last name
- Email address
- Article number / SKU (selected from a list of eligible products)
- Serial number (printed on the product's rating label)
- Purchase date
- Retailer where the product was purchased (e.g. the VONROC website,
  Amazon, bol.com, Praxis, Blokker, Obelink, AH Voordeelshop)
- Proof of purchase (uploaded as part of the form)

## After submitting

The customer receives a confirmation email within a few minutes of
submitting the registration form.

## Important

The AI assistant cannot register a product on the customer's behalf — it
can only explain the process above and point the customer to
https://vonroc.nl/productregistratie. If the customer wants help
completing registration, offer to escalate to a human colleague.
