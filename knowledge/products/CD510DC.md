# VONROC CD510DC — Cordless Drill Driver 20V

Source: VONROC Netherlands
Source page: https://vonroc.nl/accuboormachine-20v-incl-2-0ah-accu-en-snellader
Retrieved: 2026-08-20

## Platform

VONROC VPower 20V platform. Compatible with any VPower 20V 2.0Ah or 4.0Ah
battery and the VPower 20V fast charger.

## Specifications

| Spec | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Voltage | 20V |
| Speeds | 2 |
| No-load speed (setting I) | 0–350 rpm |
| No-load speed (setting II) | 0–1300 rpm |
| Max torque | 25 Nm |
| Torque settings | 19 screw settings + 1 drill setting |
| Chuck capacity | 0.8–10 mm |
| Max drilling — wood | 20 mm |
| Max drilling — metal | 6 mm |
| Max drilling — concrete | Not suitable |
| Weight (excl. battery) | 1.15 kg |

## What's in the box

2.0Ah VPower 20V battery, fast charger, 2 screw bits (PH2, PZ2), belt
clip.

## Warranty

2 years standard, 3 years if registered within 30 days of purchase (see
`../warranty.md` and `../product-registration.md`).

## Notes for troubleshooting / support

- The CD510DC is **not suitable for drilling concrete** — this is by
  design, not a defect. If a customer reports it "can't drill into
  concrete" or "the bit won't go into brick/concrete", explain this is
  expected and recommend the RH501DC rotary hammer for concrete work
  instead of treating it as a fault.
- If the tool stops shortly after starting, follow the general
  troubleshooting steps in `../troubleshooting.md` (battery charge, try an
  alternative VPower 20V battery, check for overheating) before escalating.
