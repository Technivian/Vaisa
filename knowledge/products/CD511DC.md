# VONROC CD511DC — PRO Cordless Drill Driver 20V (Brushless)

Source: VONROC Netherlands
Source page: https://vonroc.nl/pro-accuboormachine-20v-brushless-motor-40nm-incl-2-0ah-accu-en-lader
Retrieved: 2026-08-20

## Platform

VONROC VPower 20V platform. Compatible with any VPower 20V 2.0Ah or 4.0Ah
battery and the VPower 20V fast charger.

## Specifications

| Spec | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Voltage | 20V |
| Motor | Brushless |
| Speeds | 2 |
| No-load speed (setting I) | 0–450 rpm |
| No-load speed (setting II) | 0–1600 rpm |
| Max torque | 40 Nm |
| Torque settings | 17 screw settings + 1 drill setting |
| Chuck | Quick-change, 0.8–10 mm |
| Max drilling — wood | 35 mm |
| Max drilling — metal | 10 mm |
| Max drilling — concrete | Not suitable |
| Weight (excl. battery) | 1.15 kg |

## What's in the box

2.0Ah VPower 20V battery, fast charger, 2 screw bits, belt clip. (Bundles
with 4.0Ah batteries and larger accessory sets are also sold — the core
tool specifications above are the same across bundles.)

## Why "PRO" vs. the standard CD510DC

The CD511DC uses a brushless motor (no wearing carbon brushes — more
efficient, more durable, less heat, quieter) and delivers more torque and
higher no-load speeds than the CD510DC, at the same weight.

## Warranty

2 years standard, 3 years if registered within 30 days of purchase (see
`../warranty.md` and `../product-registration.md`).

## Notes for troubleshooting / support

- Like the CD510DC, the CD511DC is **not suitable for drilling concrete**
  — recommend the RH501DC rotary hammer for concrete work.
- If the tool stops shortly after starting or loses power, follow the
  general troubleshooting steps in `../troubleshooting.md` before
  escalating.
