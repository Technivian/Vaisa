# VONROC RH501DC — Cordless Rotary Hammer 20V

Source: VONROC Netherlands
Source page: https://vonroc.nl/accu-boorhamer-20v
Retrieved: 2026-08-20

## Platform

VONROC VPower 20V platform. Compatible with any VPower 20V 2.0Ah or 4.0Ah
battery (e.g. CD801AA 2.0Ah, CD803AA 4.0Ah) and the VPower 20V fast
charger. **Battery and charger are not included** in the base listing —
check the specific bundle the customer ordered.

## Specifications

| Spec | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Voltage | 20V |
| Functions | 3-in-1: rotary hammer, drill, screwdriver |
| No-load speed | 0–900 rpm |
| Impact rate | 0–5000 impacts/min |
| Impact energy | 1.3 Joule |
| Chuck | SDS-plus |
| Max drilling — concrete | 10 mm |
| Max drilling — metal | 8 mm |
| Max drilling — wood | 16 mm |
| Weight (excl. battery) | 1.2 kg |

## What's in the box

3 SDS-plus concrete drill bits, 3 HSS bits, 4 screw bits, bit holder, bit
extension, storage box, toolbag. LED work light built in.

## Warranty

2 years standard, 3 years if registered within 30 days of purchase (see
`../warranty.md` and `../product-registration.md`).

## Notes for troubleshooting / support

- This is the tool to recommend when a customer needs to drill into
  **concrete or masonry** — the CD510DC and CD511DC drills are not rated
  for that.
- If the tool stops shortly after starting or loses power, follow the
  general troubleshooting steps in `../troubleshooting.md` (battery
  charge, try an alternative VPower 20V battery, check for overheating)
  before escalating.
