# VONROC Returns

Source: VONROC Netherlands
Source page: https://vonroc.nl/retourneren
Retrieved: 2026-08-20

## Return period

Customers may return an order within **14 days** after receiving it,
provided the product is unused and returned in its original, intact
packaging.

## Exchanges

VONROC does not currently offer direct exchanges. A customer who wants a
different product returns the eligible item and places a new order.

## Return process

1. The customer submits a return request through the online return form,
   providing their order details, the reason for return, and optionally
   photos or additional information.
2. VONROC normally responds within **1–2 working days** with return
   instructions.
3. The customer sends the product back following those instructions.
4. Once VONROC has received and approved the return, the refund is paid
   back to the original payment method within **5 working days**.

## Important distinction

A defective or malfunctioning product should **not** be processed as a
standard 14-day return. Defect and warranty cases follow a separate
"Defect or guarantee" process — see `warranty.md`. If a customer describes
a product that stopped working or was damaged on arrival, treat it as a
possible warranty/defect case, not a change-of-mind return.
