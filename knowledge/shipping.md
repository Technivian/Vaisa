# VONROC Shipping

Source: VONROC Netherlands
Source page: https://vonroc.nl/verzenden
Retrieved: 2026-08-20

## Carriers

VONROC ships orders with **DHL** or **DPD**. Large or bulky items are
always shipped via DPD. Where both carriers are possible, the customer can
sometimes choose between them at checkout.

## Shipping costs

- Orders under €50: **€4.95** shipping
- Orders of €50 or more: **free shipping**

## Delivery timeline

Typical delivery is **1–2 working days** after the order is dispatched.

## Notifications and tracking

- The customer receives an order confirmation email when the order is
  placed.
- Once the package is dispatched, the customer receives a Track & Trace
  notification by email from both VONROC and the carrier.
- The day before delivery, the carrier sends an SMS or email with the
  expected delivery window; the customer can use Track & Trace to adjust
  the delivery time or location.

## Missed delivery

If nobody is home, the carrier leaves the package with a neighbor or
attempts delivery again the next working day.

## Order statuses used internally

- **Processing** — order received, being prepared for dispatch.
- **Shipped** — handed to the carrier, tracking code available.
- **Delivered** — confirmed delivered by the carrier.
- **Delayed** — dispatch or delivery taking longer than the timelines
  above.
- **Cancelled** — the order was cancelled before dispatch.
