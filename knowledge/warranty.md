# VONROC Warranty

Sources:
- VONROC customer service warranty article — https://vonroc.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/77000576345-warranty
- VONROC general terms and conditions (Algemene Voorwaarden) — https://vonroc.nl/algemene-voorwaarden
- VONROC product registration page — https://vonroc.nl/productregistratie
Retrieved: 2026-08-20

This file combines the plain-language customer-facing warranty terms with
the underlying legal warranty clause from the general terms, since the two
sources describe the same warranty from different angles and a customer
question can hinge on either.

## Standard warranty (no registration required)

- **Machines:** 2 years from the order date against material and
  manufacturing defects. This is the statutory warranty and applies
  automatically — the customer does not need to register the product to
  have it.
- **Non-wear accessories, batteries, and chargers:** 6 months against
  material and manufacturing defects.
- **Wear parts** (e.g. saw blades, sanding discs, drive belts, carbon
  brushes) are excluded from warranty — these are consumables expected to
  wear out with normal use.

## Extended warranty via product registration

Registering a product within **30 days of purchase** adds **1 extra year**
of warranty on top of the standard 2-year machine warranty — 3 years in
total. See `product-registration.md` for how to register.

## What voids the warranty

- Misuse, improper operation, or failure to maintain the product as
  described in its manual.
- Opening, disassembling, or attempting to repair the product yourself —
  this ends the customer's right to a warranty claim on that unit.
- Damage caused by non-VONROC accessories or improper installation.
- Custom-made or customer-specified products.

## Legal/general-terms detail (from the Algemene Voorwaarden)

- VONROC applies the statutory warranty period of the country of purchase;
  where no such statutory period exists, VONROC applies a 24-month
  warranty.
- The customer must notify VONROC in writing within the warranty period,
  and within 2 months of discovering the defect, with a clear description
  of the problem.
- Product specifications are provided in good faith but are not
  contractually binding; normal industry tolerances apply.
- If a claim qualifies, VONROC chooses the remedy: repair, replacement, or
  a negotiated price reduction.
- The customer generally bears return shipping costs for a warranty claim,
  unless VONROC confirms the defect.

## How a warranty/defect claim works here

1. The customer describes the issue. This assistant may ask basic,
   verified troubleshooting questions (see `troubleshooting.md`).
2. If a defect looks likely, or troubleshooting doesn't resolve it, the
   case is escalated to a human colleague, who assesses eligibility and
   decides on repair, replacement, or refund.
3. This assistant must not confirm that a specific claim is approved,
   promise a refund/replacement, or state that a specific product is or is
   not covered — only a human colleague makes that decision, using the
   terms above plus the actual product and order details.
